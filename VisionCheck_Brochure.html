Why this matters

The world's biggest treatable health crisis
that nobody talks about

2/3

Can't get glasses

Two out of three people in low-income countries who need eyeglasses don't have access to them. Not because glasses are complex — but because they've never been screened.

5%

Children screened

In Francophone Africa, only about 5% of second-graders have ever taken a vision test. Children with impaired vision are 5-7% less likely to complete school or become literate.

1:1M

Doctor ratio

Sub-Saharan Africa has roughly 1 ophthalmologist per million people. Hospitals are far away, expensive, and often in cities — while 60% of the population lives rurally.

What makes it different

Built for people who've
never had an eye test

8 screening tests

Acuity, color vision, astigmatism, contrast, near vision, macular health, peripheral vision, and symptom-based risk assessment — all from one phone.

Voice-guided in local languages

ElevenLabs AI narrates every instruction in English, French, Swahili, and Amharic. Critical for low-literacy users — voice is the primary interaction mode.

Zero hardware needed

Works on any smartphone — even entry-level $30 Android Go devices. No attachments, no internet after first load, no special equipment of any kind.

Zero literacy required

Tumbling E test (swipe direction), icon-driven symptom checker, and tap-based inputs. The entire app works without reading a single word.

AI-powered analysis

Google Gemini API analyzes all test results together, generates a plain-language health summary in the user's language, and classifies referral urgency.

Children's mode

LEA symbol tests (house, circle, square, apple) for kids and non-readers. Designed for teachers to screen an entire classroom quickly.

Share anywhere

Results shareable via WhatsApp, SMS, or downloadable PDF. A health worker can receive results even from a patient with a basic feature phone.

Smart symptom screener

Icon-based questionnaire flags conditions that can't be tested on-screen — cataracts, glaucoma risk, trachoma signs, diabetic retinopathy indicators.

Who it's for

Designed for three audiences

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Families in rural communities

Adults who've never been screened. Children falling behind in school without knowing why. Elders with worsening vision who think it's "just aging." Anyone with a shared smartphone can run a complete screening for their whole family.

🏥

Community health workers

CHWs and teachers who conduct screenings in villages and schools. Batch mode lets them screen 30 students in a session and generate one referral report. Research shows trained teachers significantly increase screening rates.

🌍

NGOs & health ministries

Organizations like Sightsavers, Orbis, and WHO AFRO need screening data to plan resources. VisionCheck provides anonymized, population-level vision data for evidence-based eye care program deployment.
